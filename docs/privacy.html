Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026-08-04

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 only when you choose “Accept analytics.” It helps measure aggregate page visits and interactions such as chapter navigation, paper-example selection, playground usage, and command copies. Analytics data may be pseudonymous rather than personally identifying.

Analytics is not loaded and no analytics page view is sent when you decline. Your choice is stored locally in your browser under rl-seminal-papers-analytics-consent.

Local preferences

The site may store completed chapter numbers locally in your browser so your progress remains available when you return. This information is not sent to the site.

Third-party services

Links to GitHub, Google Colab, Google Fonts, Google Analytics, Manning Publications, and the author’s website are third-party services with their own privacy policies. Opening those links or loading those resources may involve their own data collection.

Contact

For questions about this site, contact the project author through the author’s GitHub profile.
